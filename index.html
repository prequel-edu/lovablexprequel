Got Questions?

Do I need a laptop or can I use my phone?
Laptops are required. You'll be building a real website live during the session — you need a full keyboard and screen to make the most of it.
What if I don't have an idea yet?
That's totally okay — no idea needed to register. We'll help you discover your startup concept right at the start of the session.
Do I need to know how to code?
Zero coding experience required. Lovable's AI builds the website for you — you just describe what you want and it builds it.
Is this really free?
Yes. 100% free. No credit card, no catch. Just show up.
What do I need to join?
A laptop, a stable internet connection, and the drive to build something. We'll handle the rest.
Will I keep what I build?
Yes — you'll leave the workshop with a real, live website for your startup idea. It's yours to keep and share.
